The rate card. A new trip on a route copies these figures as its planned cost. Click a row to edit.

Where records are kept: in the Fleet Desk Records Google Sheet in your Google Drive, with a copy on each device. Changes you make with no signal wait on the device and are sent when the internet returns. The dot by the title is green when everything is synced. Use ⟳ Sync to pull the latest from your other device.

First time on a phone or PC: open ⚙ Settings, paste the Google script address and your PIN, and tap Save & connect. On a phone, use the browser menu → Add to Home screen to get an app icon.

Routine

  1. Trucks & drivers — register each truck assigned to you with its driver and phone. Picking the truck on a trip fills in the driver.
  2. Routes & rates — set each regular route once with its usual levies, tolls, gate fees and assistant pay.
  3. Plan a trip — pick the date, route, truck, container and booking. The planned costs fill in from the rate card, and you can adjust them for this trip.
  4. Set the status to In progress when the truck leaves, and enter the odometer start reading (it fills in from the truck’s last reading — check it against the dashboard).
  5. After the trip, open it, enter the odometer end reading and what was actually paid in the Actual column, then set it to Completed.

Reading it: Over / (under) is actual minus planned. Red means the trip cost more than planned. The Completed, no actuals flag on the Trips tab lists finished trips whose expenses are still missing.

The trucks and drivers are leased, so their hire is not tracked here. Expenses tracked: levies (union / park), tolls, gate fees (terminal / port), assistant pay, and other.

Trip report: every trip also goes into a separate Google Sheet, Fleet Desk – Trip Report, with the trip type (import / export), truck, driver, container, route and odometer readings — no expenses, booking references or notes. It updates by itself each time you save, and you can share that file with management or the leasing company. Open it with 📄 Trip report at the top.

Fund retirement: on the Trips tab set the From and To dates (and any truck or route filter), then tap 🧾 Fund retirement. Record the money you received for those dates; the statement lists every trip with its expenses, odometer and km, and shows Received, Spent and Balance. Print it (or save as PDF), export it to Excel, or copy the summary for WhatsApp.

WhatsApp update: press 💾 Save + 📤 WhatsApp on a trip to save it and send its status, route, truck, driver and phone, container(s), odometer and status note to your group — no money is included. 📋 Copy copies the same text to paste anywhere. For two 20ft containers on one truck, choose Size 2 × 20' and fill in the second container.

Truck approval: open a truck in the Trucks tab and set Approval to Pending, Approved or Rejected, with the decision date and the reason. A rejected truck needs a reason and can't be put on a new trip. The report's Trucks page shows each truck's approval, trips, kilometres and average km per trip.

Export CSV opens in Excel. You can also open the Google Sheet directly to see every trip as a row. Don’t edit it there while the desk is syncing.
